# Bank vs. Card Payment Pricing Requirement

## Status

**LOCKED OWNER DECISION — 2026-09-18.**

This document supersedes `docs/CASH-CARD-PRICING.md` for all new pricing behavior and customer-facing terminology.

The historical fixed-5% credit-card pricing rule must remain reproducible under its existing versioned rule id. Do **not** mutate the meaning of an existing versioned id. Implement this policy under a new versioned pricing rule.

## 1. Objective

Implement a two-price structure that encourages customers to use bank-based payment methods while accounting for credit/debit card processing costs and the additional risk associated with card disputes and chargebacks.

The **Bank Payment Price is always the actual calculated selling price of the item**.

The Bank Payment Price must never be discounted, increased, or rounded by this feature.

The standard website/card price is calculated by increasing the Bank Payment Price according to the applicable pricing tier and then applying the required retail rounding rule.

## 2. Core Pricing Logic

### Bank Payment Price

The **Bank Payment Price is the base selling price** generated by the existing product pricing calculation.

This value remains unchanged by this feature.

All existing product-cost, markup, margin-floor, minimum-profit, Group Buy tier, override, recalculation, freeze, and refund-basis logic continues to use the underlying Bank Payment Price unless a separate locked rule explicitly states otherwise.

### Regular/Card Price

Calculate the preliminary Regular/Card Price as:

**Preliminary Card Price = Bank Payment Price × (1 + Applicable Percentage)**

Then:

**Round the Preliminary Card Price UP to the next $5 increment.**

Final formula:

**Regular/Card Price = ROUND UP TO NEXT $5 [Bank Payment Price × (1 + Applicable Percentage)]**

The percentage is an internal calculation and must **NOT** be displayed to customers.

## 3. Pricing Tiers

The applicable tier must always be determined using the **Bank Payment Price**, not the inflated Regular/Card Price.

| Bank Payment Price | Card Price Increase |
| --- | ---: |
| Under $500 | **5.0%** |
| $500–$999.99 | **4.5%** |
| $1,000–$2,499.99 | **4.0%** |
| $2,500–$4,999.99 | **3.5%** |
| $5,000 and above | **3.0%** |

The card-price increase must never be less than **3%**.

Tier boundary behavior is exact:

- $499.99 and below → 5.0%
- $500.00 through $999.99 → 4.5%
- $1,000.00 through $2,499.99 → 4.0%
- $2,500.00 through $4,999.99 → 3.5%
- $5,000.00 and above → 3.0%

## 4. Rounding Rule

Only the **Regular/Card Price** is rounded by this feature.

The **Bank Payment Price must remain exactly as calculated by the existing pricing system.**

After applying the appropriate percentage increase:

### Round the Regular/Card Price UP to the next $5 increment.

Examples:

- $783.75 → **$785**
- $1,034.55 → **$1,035**
- $2,080.00 → **$2,080**
- $2,081.20 → **$2,085**
- $4,141.01 → **$4,145**
- $10,301.25 → **$10,305**

If the calculated Card Price is already an exact multiple of $5, **do not increase it further**.

Example:

$2,080.00 → **$2,080**, not $2,085.

Conceptually:

**Final Card Price = CEILING(Preliminary Card Price, $5)**

Spreadsheet equivalent:

`=CEILING(BankPaymentPrice*(1+Percentage),5)`

The implementation must use deterministic decimal/integer money arithmetic, never binary floating point.

## 6. Customer-Facing Display

Customer-facing treatment depends on surface.

### Collection and search

The discovery price is the **Bank Payment Price**.

Display conceptually:

**As low as $2,000**

*Bank Payment Price*

Rules:

- use the lowest Bank Payment Price among currently purchasable eligible variants;
- do not use an unavailable configuration to advertise a lower "As low as" price;
- collection/search cards do not need to show the Regular/Card Price alongside the discovery price.

### Buy Now product page

For the selected variant/configuration show:

**Price: $2,080**

**Bank Payment Price: $2,000**

**Save $80 with Bank Payment**

The Regular/Card Price is the primary detailed-product price.

### Cart

Show the exact Regular/Card Price and Bank Payment Price for each selected line/configuration. Cart totals are calculated per line item and then summed; never re-tier from the combined cart value.

Do NOT display the internal percentage.

Do NOT display wording such as:

- "4% discount"
- "4% cash discount"
- "4% credit card fee"
- "Credit card surcharge"

The customer should see the actual dollar prices and actual dollar savings.

Customer-facing terminology must use **Bank Payment Price**, not "cash price", "cash-equivalent price", or similar wording.

## 7. Eligible Bank Payment Methods

The Bank Payment Price applies only to approved bank/manual payment methods, including:

- Zelle
- Bank transfer
- ACH when designated as an eligible bank payment
- Wire transfer
- Other explicitly owner-approved electronic bank/manual payment methods added in the future

Standard credit/debit cards and other payment methods designated as standard payments use the Regular/Card Price.

No paper payment method is eligible for Bank Payment Price. This includes personal checks, cashier's checks, certified checks, money orders, and other paper instruments.

A payment method is not eligible merely because it is lower-cost. Eligibility must come from approved configuration/policy.

## 8. Checkout Logic

Buy Now product pages and cart use the final rounded Regular/Card Price as the primary detailed-purchase price, while also showing the lower Bank Payment Price.

Collection/search discovery may instead lead with **As low as [Bank Payment Price]** under §6.

When the customer selects an eligible Bank Payment method:

**Final amount due = Bank Payment Price**

When the customer selects a standard/card payment method:

**Final amount due = Final Rounded Regular/Card Price**

The final amount due must be clearly shown before the customer completes the order.

Implementation must verify what Shopify checkout/payment capabilities are available for the chosen plan and payment configuration. If Shopify cannot natively switch the payable amount at payment-method selection, the engineering design must provide a compliant supported mechanism or explicitly surface the platform limitation before release. Do not silently fake this behavior only on the product page/cart.

### Merchandise-only scope

Bank Payment savings apply to merchandise only. Do not include tax, shipping, separately charged insurance, duties, or other non-merchandise charges in "Save $X with Bank Payment."

### Cart aggregation

Bank/Card pricing is determined per line item/configuration. The cart does not choose a new tier from the combined subtotal.

- Cart Bank Payment Merchandise Total = sum of Bank Payment line totals.
- Cart Regular/Card Merchandise Total = sum of Regular/Card line totals.
- Cart Bank Payment Savings = Regular/Card Merchandise Total - Bank Payment Merchandise Total.

## 9. Savings Calculation

The customer-facing savings amount must be calculated AFTER the Regular/Card Price has been rounded.

Use:

**Customer Savings = Final Rounded Card Price − Bank Payment Price**

Example:

Bank Payment Price = $750

Calculated Card Price = $783.75

Rounded Card Price = $785

Therefore:

**Customer Savings = $785 − $750 = $35**

Display:

**Save $35 with Bank Payment**

Do not calculate the displayed savings from the percentage alone.

## 10. Administrative / Automation Requirements

The administrator should only need to establish the normal calculated selling price of the item.

That price automatically becomes the **Bank Payment Price**.

The system must automatically:

1. Read the calculated Bank Payment Price.
2. Determine the applicable pricing tier based exclusively on the Bank Payment Price.
3. Apply the applicable percentage increase.
4. Calculate the preliminary Regular/Card Price.
5. **Round the Regular/Card Price UP to the next $5 increment.**
6. Leave the Bank Payment Price unchanged.
7. Calculate the actual dollar savings using the final rounded Card Price.
8. Display the final rounded Card Price as the primary website price.
9. Display the Bank Payment Price as the alternative bank-payment price.
10. Display the actual dollar savings where appropriate.
11. Maintain both prices correctly through the product page, cart, and checkout.
12. Apply the Bank Payment Price when an eligible bank payment method is selected.
13. Apply the final rounded Regular/Card Price when a standard/card payment method is selected.

## 11. Final Pricing Logic

**Step 1 — Determine tier from Bank Payment Price**

**Step 2 — Preliminary Card Price = Bank Payment Price × (1 + Tier Percentage)**

**Step 3 — Final Card Price = Preliminary Card Price rounded UP to the next $5**

**Step 4 — Savings = Final Card Price − Bank Payment Price**

The **Bank Payment Price is always the underlying calculated selling price and must remain unchanged by this feature.**

## 12. Engineering / Versioning Requirements

This policy replaces the previously approved single fixed 5% card uplift for new pricing calculations.

Do not modify the semantics of the existing versioned card-price rule. Historical calculations referencing the prior rule must remain reproducible.

Implement this policy as a **new versioned card-price rule**. Recommended semantic name:

`BANK_TIERED_UPLIFT_CEIL_FIVE_DOLLARS_V1`

The new rule owns:

- tier selection from Bank Payment Price;
- the applicable tier percentage;
- exact preliminary Card Price calculation;
- ceiling to the next $5 increment;
- exact dollar savings derivation.

The underlying Bank Payment Price must be passed into the rule as an already-finalized amount and returned unchanged alongside the derived card/display values.

Customer-facing DTOs should prefer explicit names such as:

- `bankPaymentPrice`
- `regularCardPrice`
- `bankPaymentSavings`
- `groupBuyBankPaymentPrice`
- `groupBuyRegularCardPrice`
- `buyNowBankPaymentPrice`
- `buyNowRegularCardPrice`

Historical internal fields may be migrated safely rather than destructively renamed, but storefront and admin boundaries must not use ambiguous generic `price` or customer-facing "cash" terminology.

## 14. Buy Now cart mode and Bank Payment Discount eligibility — owner locked 2026-09-19

Bank Payment remains available as a payment method even when specific merchandise is not eligible for the lower Bank Payment Price.

Use a per-product/per-variant **Bank Payment Discount Eligible** flag:

- default ON for new products/variants;
- eligible line + Bank Payment mode -> Bank Payment Price;
- ineligible line + Bank Payment mode -> Regular/Card Price;
- ineligible lines do not block Bank Payment for the order.

A cart has one payment mode at a time: Card or Bank Payment.

Buy Now product page actions:

- **Add to Cart**
- **Add to Cart with Bank Payment Discount**

The Bank Payment action switches the entire cart to Bank Payment mode and reprices all eligible lines. Normal Add to Cart preserves the current cart mode.

The cart presents:

- **Card Checkout**
- **Bank Payment Checkout**

Switching modes must reprice all eligible lines and must never allow Card payment to complete at Bank Payment pricing.

This path is money-critical and requires enhanced regression/integration/tamper-resistance testing.

## 15. Buy Now Bank Payment order timing — owner locked 2026-09-19

The Buy Now Bank Payment Price is guaranteed for 24 hours after order placement.

Customer-facing disclosure must state that:

- the price is locked for 24 hours;
- after 24 hours pricing is subject to change and is not guaranteed;
- the order is not committed and item availability is not guaranteed until Bank Payment is received and verified.

After 24 hours:

- if payment is still unreceived and the underlying price is unchanged, the order may remain open;
- if payment is still unreceived and the underlying price changes by **any amount**, cancel the unpaid order and send a cancellation email.

Do not reserve Buy Now inventory before payment receipt/verification.

Bank Payment receipt is manually verified by admin. Record actual amount received, method, reference/confirmation number when available, verification timestamp, and verifying admin.

## 16. Shopify publication safety — owner locked 2026-09-19

After the real Shopify sync path passes money-critical integration testing, Bank Payment Price changes of 2% or less may auto-publish. Larger changes require human approval.

If a valid approved price cannot be synced:

- keep the last successfully published Shopify price authoritative and sellable for up to 48 hours;
- retry automatically;
- notify through email and persistent embedded-admin alert;
- do not mark synced until Shopify confirms;
- after 48 hours unresolved, make only the affected variant unavailable;
- restore automatically after successful sync.

Customers who purchase at the live price during the unresolved window are charged that valid published price. Do not automatically retro-refund if a lower price is published later.

Bulk approval is allowed for many >2% changes caused by one pricing-input event. Bulk reject is not allowed; rejection is per item/variant with a replacement override price and reason.

Temporary overrides expire on the next material pricing recalculation unless marked **Never Expire**.
