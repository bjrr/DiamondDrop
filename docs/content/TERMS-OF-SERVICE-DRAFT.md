## 3. Pricing: two prices, and what they mean

Every item shows two prices:

- The **Bank Payment Price** — what you pay using an eligible Bank Payment

- The **Regular/Card Price** — what you pay by card.

are shown before you order, and the product page states the exact dollar
difference. The difference is calculated on merchandise only; it does not
apply to tax, shipping or other non-merchandise charges.

## 5. Payment, inventory and when your order is secured

This section states mechanics that differ by purchase path. It is written to
match how the system actually behaves.

### 5.1 Card orders

Card payment is processed through Shopify's checkout. We never see or store
your full card number.

### 5.2 Buy Now with Bank Payment

- Choosing Bank Payment creates an order and shows you the exact amount and
  payment instructions. **Nothing is charged when you submit the form.**
- **The Bank Payment Price is guaranteed for 24 hours** from order placement.
- After 24 hours, an unpaid order remains open only while the price is
  unchanged. **If the price changes, the order is cancelled and we email
  you.**
- **Inventory is not reserved and your order is not secured until we receive
  and verify your payment.** Availability is not guaranteed before that.

### 5.3 Group Buy with Bank Payment

Group Buy works differently, on purpose:

- A placed Group Buy order **counts toward the campaign's public progress
  immediately, including an unpaid Bank Payment order.**
- **Counting toward progress is not the same as payment being received or
  verified.** It is not a guarantee that your order will be fulfilled.
- Public progress and the unlocked tier are **monotonic**: a later
  non-payment or cancellation does not reduce the displayed count or roll a
  tier back. This protects other participants who ordered in reliance on the
  displayed progress.
- Bank Payment for a Group Buy order has a **48-hour deadline, plus one
  automatic 48-hour extension**. After 96 hours an unpaid order may be made
  inactive. While the campaign is still open you may ask us by email to
  reactivate it; reactivation uses **the same order at its original locked
  price**.
- At campaign close, settlement uses the **final unlocked tier** and your
  **original payment basis** (bank or card). Where that produces a refund, we
  calculate it and process it after review.

## 7. Returns and cancellations

**Buy Now** purchases have a discretionary return window, summarised here and
governed in full by `/pages/returns`:

- an RMA must be requested **within 7 calendar days** of carrier-confirmed
  delivery, and the merchandise received by us **within 14 calendar days** of

- an RMA requested on **days 8–30** may receive either a refund **less a 50%
  restocking fee**, or **100% merchandise credit**;
- after **30 calendar days** there is no discretionary return.

**Return shipping is paid by you, and must be tracked and appropriately
insured.** We inspect returned merchandise before issuing any refund or
credit.

**Luxury Steals purchases are Final Sale.** No discretionary returns,
exchanges, cash refunds or merchandise-credit returns. You acknowledge this
explicitly at checkout. **Final Sale does not deny a legitimate warranty,
defect, damage, or wrong-item claim** — those follow the claim workflows
below, not the return policy.

Defect, damage, wrong-item and not-as-described claims are **not**
discretionary returns and are not subject to a restocking fee.
