# CaratForUs Coming Soon / First Access Landing Page

**Status:** LOCKED REQUIREMENTS  
**Purpose:** Pre-launch conversion landing page for social-media and paid-ad traffic.  
**Primary conversion:** Email signup for **CaratForUs First Access**.

This is a conversion-first landing page, not a full brand-story page or catalog. Keep it short, premium, fast, and easy to scan. The visitor should understand why CaratForUs is different and be able to sign up within the first viewport.

## 1. Primary Goal

The page is built around **CaratForUs First Access**.

Do **not** call the signup a newsletter.

Primary CTA:

**GET FIRST ACCESS**

Launch timing copy:

**Launching Soon**

Do not add a countdown until a real launch date is approved.

## 2. Locked Hero Positioning

Primary headline:

# Fine Jewelry Is About to Change.

The supporting message should quickly communicate that customers should not have to choose among:

- traditional jewelry retail markups;
- going overseas / to China themselves to chase better value; or
- high-pressure diamond buying that forces rushed decisions or bidding.

CaratForUs presents another option: strong value, flexible ways to buy jewelry, loose certified diamonds at wholesale prices, and support from a **U.S.-based company**.

Keep the hero copy concise. The signup must be visible immediately on both desktop and mobile.

Suggested supporting direction:

> A new way to buy fine jewelry is coming — wholesale-priced loose diamonds, exceptional jewelry value, and more ways to buy, backed by a U.S.-based company.

Do not expand this into a long manifesto.

## 3. Signup Form

### Required
- Email address
- First name
- Last name

First name and last name must be captured as separate required fields.

### Optional
- Mobile number
- Interests

The optional mobile number is stored on the Shopify customer as:

- namespace/key: `custom.first_access_mobile`
- type: `single_line_text_field`

Supplying a mobile number does **not** constitute SMS marketing consent. SMS marketing consent is a separate future capability and must not be inferred, created, or modified by this form.

Interest choices must support multi-select and remain optional:

- Loose Diamonds
- Engagement Rings
- Luxury Steals
- Group Buys
- Set Your Own Stone
- Custom Jewelry

Use fast selectable chips or checkboxes rather than a cumbersome form.

Optional interest selection must never block signup.

CTA:

**GET FIRST ACCESS**

First Access benefits may be stated concisely:

- early access before the public launch;
- first access to launch offers;
- first access to limited Luxury Steals;
- first notice/access to opening Group Buys;
- first access to loose-diamond shopping; and
- launch-only promotional offers.

Do not promise a specific discount percentage unless separately approved.

## 4. Five Compact Feature Teasers

Use five elegant visual teaser cards/tiles. These are teasers, not explanatory sections.

### Buy Loose Diamonds
Primary teaser:

**Loose Diamonds. Wholesale Prices. No Pressure.**

Supporting concept:

**Buy certified loose diamonds at wholesale prices. Browse and compare on your time.**

This card should be visually prominent.

### Luxury Steals
**Exceptional pieces. Limited quantities. Standout pricing.**

### Group Buys
**More buyers. Better buying power.**

Do not over-explain mechanics on this page.

### Set Your Own Stone
**Already have the stone? Find the setting or create your own.**

### Custom Jewelry
**Your idea. Made for you.**

Keep every card extremely concise.

## 5. Locked Diamond-Shopping Message

Use:

# Your Diamond. Your Decision. Your Time.

Core promise:

**Buy loose certified diamonds at wholesale prices. Browse. Compare. Decide on your time.**

Supporting line:

**No bidding. No pressure. No games.**

The purpose is to emphasize that customers can browse, compare, and make a decision without bidding pressure or rushed purchasing.

Do not frame the feature as a diamond auction.

This message may be integrated into the loose-diamond teaser area rather than becoming a large separate section if that keeps the page cleaner.

## 6. Overseas / U.S.-Based Disruption Message

Use a short disruption message rather than a long comparison section.

Headline direction:

# Why Go Overseas for a Better Deal?

Supporting direction:

> You shouldn't have to overpay at traditional retail, go overseas just to find better value, or rush into a diamond decision.

> CaratForUs gives you another option — exceptional value from a U.S.-based company.

The intent is to challenge the belief that customers must personally buy overseas / from China to obtain better pricing.

Do **not**:
- attack Chinese manufacturers;
- turn the page into an anti-China message;
- create a long China-versus-U.S. comparison table; or
- make unsupported quality claims.

The message is simply that customers no longer have to go overseas themselves to chase better value.

A concise trust line such as the following is acceptable:

**U.S.-based. Certified diamonds. Built around better value.**

Do not create a separate large trust-badge section.

## 7. Page Structure

Keep the page intentionally lean.

Preferred flow:

1. Hero
2. Immediate First Access signup
3. Five compact visual feature teasers
4. Short disruption / U.S.-based message
5. Final First Access signup

Do not make visitors weed through large amounts of copy before signing up.

If the diamond message can be integrated cleanly into the feature area, do that instead of creating a large extra section.

The page should be optimized for visitors arriving directly from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and paid ads.

## 8. Launch-Deal Teaser

The approved concept is:

**The Best Launch Deals Won't Wait.**

This is optional and must remain lightweight.

If it improves conversion without clutter, show at most 2–3 tasteful mystery/locked deal teasers such as:

- First Access Reveal
- Limited Quantity
- Opening Offer

If it makes the page feel crowded, omit the cards and retain only a short launch-deal line near signup.

Conversion simplicity takes priority.

## 9. Final Signup

Repeat the First Access signup once near the bottom.

Visitors should not need to scroll back to the hero to join.

Use the same signup data model and CTA:

**GET FIRST ACCESS**

## 10. Post-Signup Sharing

Do **not** build a referral program.

No:
- points;
- referral codes;
- reward ladders;
- waitlist ranking; or
- "invite three friends to move up the list" mechanics.

After successful signup, offer optional sharing:

**You're in. Know someone who'd love CaratForUs?**

**Share First Access with a friend.**

Provide simple sharing options where supported:

- Text / SMS
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Copy Link

Optional native social sharing may be added only if it is clean and useful.

Suggested share-copy direction:

> CaratForUs is launching soon — wholesale-priced loose diamonds, Luxury Steals, Group Buys, custom jewelry and more. I thought you'd want First Access too.

Sharing is optional and must not affect signup status.

## 11. Visual Direction

The page must feel:

**Modern luxury first. Disruptive value second.**

Use:

- high-end jewelry imagery;
- macro diamond/ring photography;
- elegant typography;
- generous whitespace;
- sophisticated dark or premium-neutral treatment consistent with the existing CaratForUs brand;
- subtle motion only where it adds polish;
- jewelry-first visuals; and
- excellent mobile composition.

Do not make the page look like:

- a coupon site;
- a liquidation site;
- a discount marketplace;
- a cluttered startup landing page; or
- a wall of text.

Avoid excessive sale-red badges, flashing banners, popups, and gimmicks.

Desired reaction:

> "This looks like luxury jewelry — but they're changing how it's bought."

## 12. Mobile / Social-Traffic Requirements

Assume a significant share of traffic comes from social media and paid mobile ads.

Therefore:

- signup must be visible immediately on mobile;
- the page must load quickly;
- copy must be easy to scan;
- buttons/tap targets must be comfortably sized;
- no horizontal overflow;
- no tiny text;
- no long forms;
- optimize image assets for performance;
- do not require account creation; and
- email signup should take only a few seconds.

## 13. Shopify / Architecture Rules

Follow the existing CaratForUs **Shopify-native-first** architecture principle.

Before creating anything custom, inspect what the current theme/store already supports.

Prefer Shopify-native customer/email capture capabilities where practical.

Do not create a second customer database or a new custom CRM for this landing page.

Store optional interests using the simplest durable mechanism compatible with the existing Shopify setup, such as Shopify customer tags/metafields or the existing approved marketing integration.

Do not add unnecessary backend architecture.

Do not disturb:

- Set Your Own Stone;
- existing product/catalog data;
- Bank/Card pricing;
- Shopify checkout;
- production StoneSessions;
- policy pages; or
- existing navigation/business logic.

This is a landing-page build, not a platform rewrite.

10. Successful signup produces a clear success state.
11. Share-with-a-friend options work.
