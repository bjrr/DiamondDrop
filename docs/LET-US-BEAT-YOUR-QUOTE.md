# Let Us Beat Your Quote! — MVP1 Locked Specification

## Status
**LOCKED MVP1 REQUIREMENT**

This document defines the approved CaratForUs **Let Us Beat Your Quote!** acquisition feature for Custom Jewelry and competing jewelry offers. It controls implementation of this feature unless the owner explicitly changes the policy.

## Purpose
CaratForUs should actively compete for customers who are considering custom jewelry or jewelry offers from other jewelers, including overseas sellers.

The feature should be presented under the customer-facing headline:

**Let Us Beat Your Quote!**

The objective is to let a customer submit a genuine competing offer and give CaratForUs the opportunity to match or beat the jewelry price while preserving CaratForUs's own production timeline, warranty, return/cancellation policy, shipping terms, and other service terms.

## Core Principle: What CaratForUs Matches
CaratForUs matches or beats the **price of the jewelry itself**, based on a materially comparable jewelry design and specifications.

CaratForUs is **not** required to match the competing seller's:
- production lead time or delivery date;
- return or cancellation policy;
- warranty;
- shipping method or shipping timeline;
- payment terms;
- promotions, coupons, rewards, or other non-product incentives;
- other service terms.

If CaratForUs makes an offer, CaratForUs will define and disclose its own estimated production/delivery timeline, warranty, return/cancellation policy, shipping terms, and other applicable purchase terms.

## Like-for-Like Jewelry Comparison
A qualifying comparison must give CaratForUs enough information to determine that the jewelry is materially the same or substantially comparable in the specifications that drive construction and value.

The comparison should include, where applicable:
- design/CAD/rendering or dimensioned design image;
- jewelry type and construction;
- metal type;
- metal purity;
- metal color;
- finished or estimated metal weight when available;
- overall dimensions;
- ring size, bracelet/necklace length, or other finished sizing;
- center-stone type, natural/lab status, shape, dimensions, carat weight, color, clarity, cut/quality, and certification when applicable;
- side/accent stone type, natural/lab status, shape, dimensions, quantity, quality, and total carat weight when applicable;
- setting style and material construction details;
- engraving/personalization included in the competing design;
- other specifications necessary to establish a meaningful like-for-like comparison.

CaratForUs may decline guarantee eligibility when the submitted information is insufficient to establish comparability.

## Eligible Submission Types

### 1. Recent Custom Jewelry Quote — Guarantee Eligible When Qualified
A custom jewelry quote can qualify for the full guarantee when:
- it was issued within the **7 calendar days immediately preceding submission to CaratForUs**;
- the quote date is visible or otherwise verifiable;
- the seller is identifiable and verifiable;
- the quoted price is verifiable;
- the customer provides the CAD/design specification package needed for like-for-like comparison;
- the quote is genuine and available to the customer;
- CaratForUs can verify the offer and material specifications.

For a verified qualifying quote issued within 7 calendar days, CaratForUs will:
1. match or beat the qualifying jewelry price; **or**
2. if CaratForUs cannot match or beat it, provide the customer **10% off one eligible CaratForUs purchase, up to a maximum discount of $100**.

The 10%-off benefit is the fallback outcome of the same guarantee, not a separate customer election.

### 2. Active Online Listed Item — Guarantee Eligible When Qualified
A customer may submit an item currently offered for sale on a verifiable jewelry seller's website.

To qualify:
- the customer must provide the live product URL;
- the item must be active and actually available to purchase when CaratForUs verifies it;
- the seller must be identifiable and verifiable;
- the current advertised jewelry price must be verifiable;
- the listing and supporting materials must provide enough specifications to establish a materially comparable item.

A live online listing does **not** need to have been created within the last 7 days. The relevant condition is that the price is live and available when CaratForUs verifies it.

For a verified qualifying active online item, CaratForUs will:
1. match or beat the qualifying jewelry price; **or**
2. if CaratForUs cannot match or beat it, provide the customer **10% off one eligible CaratForUs purchase, up to a maximum discount of $100**.

CaratForUs should record its own evidence of the live offer at verification time, including URL, seller, price, verification timestamp, relevant specifications, and a snapshot or screenshot where practical. Customer-provided screenshots alone do not establish eligibility.

### 3. Older Custom Jewelry Quote — Review Without Guarantee
CaratForUs should **accept custom quotes older than 7 calendar days** because they can still represent valuable business.

For a quote older than 7 days:
- CaratForUs will still review the quote and may voluntarily match or beat it;
- CaratForUs is under **no obligation** to match or beat the price;
- the **10%-off, up-to-$100 fallback does not apply**;
- current material and market pricing may be used to formulate CaratForUs's offer.

There is no need to reject an older quote solely because of age.

Customer-facing message should make the distinction clear, for example:

> Your quote is more than 7 days old, but we'd still love the opportunity to beat it. Because precious-metal and gemstone prices can change, older quotes aren't eligible for our guaranteed match-or-beat offer or the 10%-off fallback. Submit it anyway and we'll review it using today's pricing to see what we can do.

### 4. Competing Group Buy — Review Without Guarantee
A customer may submit an active Group Buy from another jeweler.

For a competing Group Buy:
- the Group Buy must be currently active and available;
- the customer should provide the live URL and any available specifications;
- CaratForUs may review the design and price and choose to match or beat it;
- there is **no guaranteed match-or-beat outcome**;
- there is **no 10%-off fallback benefit** if CaratForUs cannot match or beat it.

Repeated customer interest in the same outside Group Buy may be used as a demand signal for a future CaratForUs Group Buy.

## Overseas Offers Are Explicitly Eligible
The feature intentionally targets customers considering overseas jewelry sellers as well as U.S. sellers.

A competing seller does not have to be U.S.-based.

CaratForUs compares the quoted or advertised **jewelry price itself** for the materially comparable piece. CaratForUs should not artificially add hypothetical import duties, customs charges, or other assumed costs to the competing jewelry price merely to create a more favorable comparison.

CaratForUs must not participate in, encourage, or advise on inaccurate customs declarations, misclassification, undervaluation, or false gift declarations by any seller or customer.

## Verification and Fraud Protection
Submission does not equal verification and does not obligate CaratForUs to honor a price.

CaratForUs may independently verify:
- seller identity;
- seller website/contact information;
- quote or listing authenticity;
- quote date;
- current availability;
- advertised/quoted price;
- CAD/design specifications;
- metal and stone specifications;
- any other fact needed to determine eligibility.

CaratForUs may request additional documentation or seller contact information and may contact the quoting seller when reasonably necessary.

The customer must certify that submitted quotes, listings, CADs, specifications, screenshots, images, and other evidence are authentic and unaltered.

The following do not qualify for the guarantee:
- fabricated quotes;
- altered or materially edited quotes;
- AI-generated fake evidence;
- forged or manipulated screenshots/documents;
- unverifiable sellers or prices;
- insufficient design/specification evidence;
- obvious pricing errors;
- private-party or used jewelry offers;
- auction pricing;
- counterfeit goods;
- wholesale/trade-only offers unavailable to the customer;
- expired or otherwise unavailable offers represented as current.

An uploaded screenshot by itself does not establish eligibility.

## Required Competitor-Service Information
The submission flow should ask the customer for information about the competing seller's service terms so CaratForUs can provide a transparent comparison. These terms are **not** terms CaratForUs is promising to match.

Ask, where known:
- What production or delivery timeline did the other jeweler quote?
- What return or cancellation policy did the other jeweler offer?
- What warranty did the other jeweler offer?
- Is the return/warranty/timeline information shown in the submitted offer, on the seller's website, or elsewhere?

Each question should support **I don't know** where appropriate. Warranty may also support **None** where appropriate.

Competitor terms displayed by CaratForUs should be labeled according to the evidence source, such as:
- **Based on information submitted by customer**; or
- **Based on seller information reviewed on [date]**.

## CaratForUs Offer Comparison
When practical, the final offer should clearly separate:
- competing jewelry price;
- CaratForUs jewelry price;
- competitor stated timeline;
- CaratForUs estimated timeline;
- competitor stated return/cancellation policy;
- CaratForUs applicable return/cancellation policy;
- competitor stated warranty;
- CaratForUs applicable warranty;
- other material differences.

The competitor's service terms do not affect whether CaratForUs must copy those terms. The price comparison is for the jewelry itself.

## Customer-Facing Entry Flow
Customer-facing headline:

**Let Us Beat Your Quote!**

Suggested supporting message:

> Already have a jewelry quote or found a piece online? Send us the design specifications and price. For qualifying verified offers, we'll match it or beat it. If we can't, we'll give you 10% off an eligible CaratForUs purchase, up to $100.

The first question should be:

**What would you like us to beat?**
- A custom jewelry quote
- An item currently for sale online
- A Group Buy

The system should determine eligibility from the submitted information rather than requiring the customer to classify their own guarantee status.

## 10%-Off Fallback Rules
When the full guarantee applies and CaratForUs cannot match or beat the qualifying verified jewelry price:
- benefit = **10% off one eligible CaratForUs purchase**;
- maximum savings = **$100**;
- one-time use;
- non-transferable;
- no cash value;
- not stackable with other promotional codes unless expressly authorized;
- intended for regular Buy Now merchandise or Custom Jewelry;
- **Group Buys and Luxury Steals are excluded** from the fallback benefit unless the owner later changes this rule.

A fake, altered, unverifiable, non-comparable, or otherwise non-qualifying submission does **not** trigger the fallback benefit.

## Required Acknowledgment Modals / Explicit Agreement
Material terms must be presented through branded explicit acknowledgment modals or equivalent required confirmation UI at the relevant step. Do not rely only on passive policy text or buried Terms & Conditions.

Do not show all acknowledgments back-to-back without context. Trigger each at the relevant decision point, and use a final Review & Agree step before submission/purchase.

### Acknowledgment 1 — What We Match
Required before a guarantee-eligible submission is finalized:

> I understand CaratForUs is matching or beating the price of the jewelry itself based on materially comparable design and specifications. CaratForUs is not required to match the competing seller's production timeline, return policy, warranty, shipping terms, payment terms, promotions, or other services.

Required action: **I Understand & Agree**

### Acknowledgment 2 — Authenticity and Verification
Required before submission:

> I confirm the quote, listing, CAD, specifications, images, and seller information I submitted are authentic and have not been altered, fabricated, or AI-generated. I understand CaratForUs may request additional documentation or independently verify the seller, offer, specifications, and price.

Required action: **I Confirm**

### Acknowledgment 3A — 7-Day Guarantee Eligibility
For a qualifying recent custom quote:

> I understand that only verified custom quotes issued within the last 7 calendar days qualify for the guaranteed match-or-beat offer and the 10%-off fallback benefit.

Required action: **I Understand**

### Acknowledgment 3B — Older Quote Review
For a custom quote older than 7 calendar days:

> This quote is more than 7 days old. CaratForUs will still review it and may offer a competitive price, but there is no guarantee that CaratForUs will match or beat it and the 10%-off fallback does not apply.

Required action: **Submit for Review Anyway**

### Acknowledgment 3C — Competing Group Buy
For a competing Group Buy:

> I understand CaratForUs may review this Group Buy and may choose to match or beat it, but there is no guaranteed match-or-beat outcome and no 10%-off fallback if CaratForUs cannot match or beat the price.

Required action: **Submit Group Buy for Review**

### Acknowledgment 4 — CaratForUs Terms Apply
Required before customer accepts a CaratForUs offer:

> I understand that if I purchase from CaratForUs, CaratForUs will provide its own production timeline, warranty, return/cancellation policy, shipping terms, and other applicable purchase terms. These terms may be different from the competing seller's terms.

Required action: **I Agree**

### Acknowledgment 5 — Final Custom Order Approval
Required before checkout for a resulting custom order:

> I have reviewed and approved the CaratForUs design, specifications, price, estimated production timeline, warranty, return/cancellation terms, and other order details shown for this custom order.

Required action: **Approve & Continue to Checkout**

## Acknowledgment Evidence Requirements
For every required acknowledgment, retain:
- exact acknowledgment text;
- acknowledgment/version ID;
- customer/account/order/project/submission reference;
- timestamp;
- applicable competitor offer reference;
- relevant CaratForUs offer/specification version;
- acceptance action;
- audit history.

Acknowledgments must not be pre-checked or silently inferred.

## Beat Definition
For MVP1, **beat** means CaratForUs offers a jewelry price that is lower than the qualifying competitor jewelry price. No minimum dollar or percentage amount below the competitor price is promised unless explicitly changed later.

## Implementation Principle
MVP1 review is manual. Do not build an automated engine that commits CaratForUs to matching or beating a competitor based solely on uploaded documents or scraped prices.

The site should automate intake, age calculation, evidence capture, acknowledgment/version storage, and workflow status where practical, while a CaratForUs staff reviewer makes the eligibility/comparability/pricing decision.

## Acceptance Cases
At minimum, implementation and QA must cover:
1. Verified custom quote submitted on Day 7 -> guarantee eligible if all other requirements pass.
2. Verified custom quote submitted on Day 8 -> accepted for review, but no guarantee and no 10%-off fallback.
3. 14-day-old legitimate quote -> accepted and reviewable; CaratForUs may voluntarily beat it without creating guarantee obligation.
4. Active verifiable online listing -> can qualify regardless of listing age if currently purchasable and comparable.
5. Customer screenshot with no independently verifiable live offer -> screenshot alone does not establish eligibility.
6. Altered/fake/AI-generated quote -> rejected from guarantee and does not trigger fallback benefit.
7. Qualifying verified quote CaratForUs can beat -> lower CaratForUs jewelry price fulfills guarantee; no fallback benefit issued.
8. Qualifying verified quote CaratForUs cannot match/beat -> 10%-off eligible purchase benefit issued, capped at $100.
9. Competing active Group Buy -> accepted for discretionary review but no guaranteed match/beat and no fallback benefit.
10. Competitor has faster production, more generous return policy, or different warranty -> does not obligate CaratForUs to copy those terms.
11. Customer must affirm authenticity and material comparison terms before submission.
12. Resulting custom order cannot proceed to checkout until the customer explicitly approves CaratForUs design/specifications/price/timeline/warranty/return-cancellation terms.
13. Duplicate/retried benefit issuance must not create multiple fallback discounts for one qualifying outcome.
