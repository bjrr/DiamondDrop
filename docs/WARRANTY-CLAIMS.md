# 1-Year Limited Manufacturing Warranty & Claims — MVP1 LOCKED SPECIFICATION

## Status
**LOCKED MVP1 REQUIREMENT — OWNER APPROVED 2026-09-13**

This document is authoritative for the CaratForUs 1-year limited manufacturing warranty claim workflow across Buy Now, Community Group Buy, Custom Jewelry, and Luxury Steals where the warranty applies. It supplements the warranty summary in `README.md`; where workflow/remedy details conflict, this document controls.

## 1. Warranty Coverage
CaratForUs provides a **1-year limited manufacturing warranty** covering qualifying defects in materials, construction, setting, or assembly under normal use, including examples such as defective solder joints, structural workmanship failures, setting defects, and defective clasps/mechanisms.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, scratches, dents, impact damage, bent/worn prongs caused by use, stone loss caused by damage or wear, misuse, improper care, sizing changes, unauthorized third-party repairs/modifications, loss, theft, or other damage not caused by a covered manufacturing defect.

Final Sale status, including Luxury Steals Final Sale status, does not automatically eliminate a legitimate covered warranty claim.

## 2. Warranty Claim Form — Required MVP1 Feature
Customers seeking warranty service must use a dedicated **Warranty Claim Form** or equivalent CaratForUs claim intake flow.

Capture or derive at least:
- order number;
- purchaser name and email/contact information;
- customer/account ID when available;
- exact item/line item and configuration;
- purchase and carrier-confirmed delivery date where available;
- date the issue was first noticed;
- description of the problem;
- photo uploads;
- video upload where practical/useful;
- whether the jewelry experienced an impact, accident, unusual force, or other damage event;
- whether the jewelry has been resized, repaired, altered, serviced, or modified by anyone after delivery, and details if yes;
- any relevant certificate/product/serial identifier;
- customer certification that submitted information is accurate;
- acknowledgment that **submission of a claim does not itself establish warranty coverage**;
- claim submission timestamp;
- claim/reference number and status;
- internal review notes/evidence/decision/audit history.

## 3. Authorization Before Shipment
A customer must not send jewelry to CaratForUs until CaratForUs reviews the claim and provides authorization/instructions for inspection.

An approved claim for **inspection** means CaratForUs has authorized the item to be sent for evaluation. It does not mean CaratForUs has already determined that the issue is a covered manufacturing defect.

## 4. Customer-Paid Shipping to CaratForUs — LOCKED
For an authorized warranty-claim inspection:
- **the customer pays the cost of shipping the jewelry to CaratForUs**;
- shipment must include **tracking**;
- shipment must be **insured for the appropriate value of the jewelry**;
- customer must follow CaratForUs shipping instructions;
- the claim record should retain tracking and insurance evidence/reference where available.

Do not advertise or promise a prepaid inbound warranty shipping label unless a later owner-approved exception/policy provides one.

## 5. Inspection and Coverage Decision
After CaratForUs receives the item, CaratForUs inspects it and determines whether the issue is covered by the limited manufacturing warranty.

The claim record should retain, as applicable:
- received date/time;
- package condition;
- item condition;
- photos/video;
- measurements/weight/stone or product identifiers where relevant;
- comparison to order/QC/specification records;
- reviewer identity;
- coverage decision;
- reason for approval/denial;
- remedy decision;
- communications;
- timestamps and audit history.

## 6. Remedy — CaratForUs Determines Appropriate Resolution
After inspection, CaratForUs determines the appropriate remedy for a valid covered claim. CaratForUs may choose, as appropriate under the circumstances:
- **repair**;
- **replacement**;
- **refund**; or
- another reasonable resolution when required/appropriate.

The customer does not automatically choose the remedy. Remedy decisions remain subject to applicable law and payment-platform/network requirements.

## 7. Local Jeweler Option — INTERNAL ONLY
CaratForUs may investigate whether an approved/reasonable jeweler local to the customer can perform an authorized repair when that is operationally or economically appropriate.

This is an **internal service option**, not a customer-facing warranty promise or entitlement.

Requirements:
- do not advertise or promise a local-jeweler repair before CaratForUs has received/reviewed the claim and determined an appropriate path;
- CaratForUs may research/select a reasonable local jeweler after claim review;
- customer must receive specific CaratForUs authorization/instructions before third-party repair work is performed;
- unauthorized third-party repair expenses are not automatically reimbursable;
- unauthorized third-party work may affect warranty coverage to the extent the work causes, contributes to, obscures, or prevents evaluation of the claimed issue, subject to applicable law;
- retain jeweler selection/authorization, approved scope/cost, communications, invoice/payment/reimbursement evidence, and completion evidence where this option is used.

## 8. Claim Statuses
MVP1 should support a simple auditable workflow such as:
- Submitted
- Under Review
- More Information Requested
- Authorized for Inspection
- In Transit to CaratForUs
- Received / Inspection Pending
- Approved — Covered Claim
- Denied — Not Covered
- Repair / Replacement / Refund in Progress
- Resolved
- Closed

Exact customer-facing labels may be refined without changing the substantive policy.

## 9. Customer-Facing Principles
Warranty information must accurately communicate that:
- the warranty is limited to covered manufacturing defects;
- a claim form and authorization are required before sending the item;
- claim submission/inspection authorization does not itself establish coverage;
- customer pays tracked, appropriately insured shipping to CaratForUs for an authorized warranty inspection;
- CaratForUs evaluates the item after receipt;
- for a valid covered claim, CaratForUs determines whether repair, replacement, refund, or another appropriate remedy applies.

Do **not** disclose or promise the internal local-jeweler option as a standard customer entitlement. If CaratForUs chooses that option for a specific resolved/approved claim, it may then communicate the specific authorized arrangement to that customer.

## 10. Evidence / Dispute Defensibility
Retain the claim form, uploads, authorization, tracking/insurance evidence, receipt/inspection evidence, coverage decision/reason, remedy decision, repair/replacement/refund references, customer communications, local-jeweler records if used, manual exceptions and complete audit history.

Do not claim the warranty or its exclusions override rights required by applicable law, card-network rules, or payment-processor rules.

## 11. MVP1 Acceptance Cases
1. Customer can submit a warranty claim with order/item/problem information and photos.
2. Claim submission does not automatically mark the claim covered.
3. Customer cannot be instructed to send the item before authorization for inspection.
4. Authorized inspection instructions state customer-paid, tracked, appropriately insured inbound shipping.
5. Receipt and inspection are recorded before final coverage/remedy determination.
6. Valid covered claim can be assigned repair, replacement, or refund by CaratForUs.
7. Luxury Steals Final Sale status does not automatically deny a legitimate warranty claim.
8. Local-jeweler option is not exposed as a promised customer entitlement before CaratForUs chooses it for a specific claim.
9. Unauthorized local repair is not automatically reimbursable.
10. Duplicate/retried remedy processing cannot create duplicate customer value.
11. Claim evidence and audit history are retrievable for support/dispute review.

## 12. Not Yet Promised
Do not invent or advertise without owner approval:
- guaranteed warranty turnaround time;
- guaranteed repair rather than replacement/refund;
- prepaid inbound warranty shipping;
- automatic reimbursement for customer-selected jewelers;
- post-warranty paid repair service;
- blanket coverage for wear/accidental damage.
